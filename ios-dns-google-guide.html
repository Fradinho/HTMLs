iOS DNS Guide

Change DNS to Google on iOS

Speed up browsing and improve privacy by switching to Google's public DNS on your iPhone or iPad.

Google's DNS Addresses

IPv4

Primary
8.8.8.8
Secondary
8.8.4.4

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. 1
    Open Settings

    Tap the Settings app on your home screen.

  2. 2
    Go to Wi-Fi

    Tap Wi-Fi to see your available networks.

    Settings → Wi-Fi
  3. 3
    Open your network details

    Tap the ⓘ icon next to your connected Wi-Fi network.

  4. 4
    Scroll down to DNS

    Scroll down until you see the DNS section, then tap Configure DNS.

  5. 5
    Switch to Manual

    Select Manual at the top of the screen instead of Automatic.

  6. 6
    Remove existing servers

    Tap the red – button next to each existing DNS entry and tap Delete to remove them.

  7. 7
    Add Google's DNS servers

    Tap Add Server and enter 8.8.8.8, then tap Add Server again and enter 8.8.4.4.

  8. 8
    Save

    Tap Save in the top-right corner. Your device will now use Google DNS.

Tip: These settings apply only to the selected Wi-Fi network. Repeat the steps for each network you use, or use a VPN or DNS profile for system-wide DNS changes.

Note: DNS settings are per Wi-Fi network on iOS. Cellular data uses your carrier's DNS and cannot be changed through Settings without a configuration profile.

Google Public DNS — 8.8.8.8 & 8.8.4.4 — is free and does not require an account.
